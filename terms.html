Recipes, plans and food safety

Aglio's recipes, plans, cooking times, portion sizes, costs and nutrition figures are there to help you plan and cook. They are estimates made with care, not guarantees.

  • Allergens. Aglio does not tell you whether a recipe or a product is free from any allergen. If anyone you cook for has an allergy or intolerance, check every recipe and read the label on every product yourself. Telling Aglio you never eat a food leaves out recipes that list it, but Aglio doesn't check what is in the products you buy or how they were made, so it is not a safeguard for an allergy.
