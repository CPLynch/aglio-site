Your supermarket

Aglio is an independent app, not run or endorsed by any supermarket. Supermarket names are used only to say where your shopping can be done.

To see products and fill a basket you sign in to your supermarket on its own website, inside the app. Your use of that website is under the supermarket's terms, and it's for you to make sure you're happy to use Aglio with your account. Aglio never sees your supermarket password.
  • Your order is with the supermarket. Aglio doesn't sell groceries. When you check out, your contract is with the supermarket, which sets the prices, offers, availability, substitutions, delivery and refunds.
