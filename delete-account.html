Delete your account

Deleting your Aglio account removes it and everything it holds. It can't be undone.

In the app

Open Account (the chef's hat at the top of the Plan screen), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. It takes effect straight away.

Without the app

Email hello@aglio.app from the Google account you sign in to Aglio with, with the subject "Delete my Aglio account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. We can only act on a request from the account's own address, since that's how we know it's yours.

What's deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • If you were your household's only member: its recipes, plans and their history, shopping lists, product choices, cupboard, preferences, favourites and photos.
  • If others remain in your household: your membership. The household's shared data stays with them, including recipes you added, as they're the household's.
  • Your usage counts for Aglio AI, apart from the last 31 days' (below).

What isn't

  • An Aglio Plus subscription's purchase. Google Play runs it and keeps its record. Deleting your account from the app stops a subscription you pay for from renewing, so Google Play won't charge you for it again. You can also cancel it yourself: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • When your household's free fortnight of Aglio Plus ran, kept with one-way hashes of your Google account identifier and of your phone's identifier for Aglio, for a year after the fortnight ended, so that it isn't had again by deleting the account and signing in again. Nothing else of the household is kept with them, and a household that never had its fortnight leaves nothing.
  • Your Aglio AI usage counts from the last 31 days, kept with the same one-way hashes, so that the allowances aren't had again by deleting the account and signing in again. Counts older than 31 days are no longer used and are cleared out.
  • Product picks Aglio AI made for ingredients, which are kept for every household and don't say who asked.
  • Error reports already sent to Sentry, which hold no account details and expire after 90 days.
  • Your supermarket account and anything in it. Aglio never held it.

The privacy policy has the full picture of what Aglio keeps.
