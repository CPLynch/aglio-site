Dinners planned, the list written, your supermarket basket filled.

Aglio is a meal-planning app for UK households. It plans dinners from your own recipes and its built-in recipe book, writes the shopping list in your supermarket's own products and prices, and puts it all in your supermarket basket for you to check out as usual.

One household shares one recipe book, one plan and one shopping list, on every phone.
